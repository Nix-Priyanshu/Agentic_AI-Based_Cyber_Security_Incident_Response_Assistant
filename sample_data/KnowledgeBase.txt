EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
====================================================================

On 07 August 2026, the Security Operations Center detected suspicious
activity originating from an employee workstation in the Finance
department. Multiple failed login attempts were followed by a successful
authentication from an unusual external IP address.

Shortly after authentication, PowerShell executed an encoded command,
downloaded a malicious payload, established communication with a remote
Command and Control (C2) server, and exfiltrated sensitive financial
documents outside the corporate network.

The incident is believed to have started from a phishing email carrying
a disguised executable attachment.

Overall Severity: CRITICAL
